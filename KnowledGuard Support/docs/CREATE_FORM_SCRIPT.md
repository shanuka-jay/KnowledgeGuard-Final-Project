# Auto-Generate Your Google Form (Multi-Quarter Edition)

You don't need to type out all the questions manually! The new KnowledgeGuard Assessment is designed to rotate questions every quarter to prevent survey fatigue and allow our Bias Engine to detect inconsistencies.

Instead of a static script, **you now generate the correct script directly from the KnowledgeGuard App!**

## Step 1: Get the Script from KnowledgeGuard
1. Log into KnowledgeGuard as an HR Admin or Analyst.
2. Navigate to **Research & Export**.
3. Under **Step 1: Prepare Google Forms**, find the **Interactive Quarterly Form Studio**.
4. Select the quarter you are running (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4).
5. Click **Copy Google Apps Script**.

## Step 2: Open Google Apps Script
1. Go to [script.google.com](https://script.google.com) (make sure you are logged into your Google account).
2. Click the **"New Project"** button in the top left.

## Step 3: Paste & Run the Code
1. Delete any existing code in the editor, and paste the code you copied from KnowledgeGuard.
2. Click the **Save** icon (the floppy disk).
3. Click the **Run** button at the top of the screen.
4. Google will ask for Permissions (Review Permissions -> Choose your account -> Advanced -> Go to Untitled Project -> Allow).
5. Look at the **Execution Log** at the bottom of the screen. It will print out an **Editor URL**.
6. Click the **Editor URL** link to open your brand new, perfectly formatted Google Form!

*(Once the form is created, you can follow the other guide to attach the Webhook to it!)*
